Grade 1-2 Learning Project

Montessori Spelling Workbook Builder

Build and print workbook pages with Blue Series and Green Series word combinations, followed by Sight Words practice pages.

Custom Combination Setup

One line per combination using: Series|Combination|sample1,sample2|Rows(optional)
Examples:
Blue|sh|ship,shop,shell|12
Green|oa|boat,coat,goat (uses selected rows per page)
New words are generated from an external comprehensive English dictionary plus fallback phonics rules.

Tip: Edit word lists in script.js to fully match your school sequence.
